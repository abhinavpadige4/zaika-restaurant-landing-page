Meet Our Chefs

Chef Rajesh Kumar

Executive Chef

With over 15 years of experience in authentic Indian cuisine, Chef Rajesh specializes in North Indian dishes and brings traditional recipes from Punjab to our kitchen.

Chef Priya Sharma

Sous Chef

Chef Priya is a master of South Indian cuisine, known for her expertise in dosas, idlis, and aromatic curries that capture the essence of Kerala and Tamil Nadu.

Chef Arjun Patel

Pastry Chef

Chef Arjun creates traditional Indian sweets and desserts, from gulab jamun to kulfi, using age-old techniques passed down through generations.
